Using the atlas

  1. Choose a building system. Start with the whole house, or select a system to uncover its parts. Use Detail view to switch between assemblies.
  2. Turn the model. Drag with a mouse or one finger. Scroll or pinch to zoom. The arrow and zoom buttons also work with a keyboard.
  3. Select a piece. Click or tap its surface to read about it. The term index finds small or hidden pieces by name.
  4. Look more closely. Isolate this part shows the selected term on its own. Show assembly puts it back in context.

The atlas illustrates common North American wood-frame construction. Cutaways omit sections to expose connections. Dimensions and spacing are illustrative.
